## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the rules of Long Backgammon?
Long Backgammon (Nardi) is played with 15 checkers on a 24-point board. Unlike short backgammon, there is no hitting (checkers cannot be knocked off). Only one checker can be taken from the "head" (starting point) per turn, except for certain initial doubles (3:3, 4:4, 6:6). Checkers move counter-clockwise around the board toward your home quarter to be borne off.

### How can I change the board theme?
Open the game and click the Settings icon (or press `Cmd + ,`). You can choose between **Classic Wood** and **Elegant Marble**, toggle movement trajectories (straight or arc), and customize animation speed.

### Can I play with a friend on the same Mac?
Yes! Use the **Pass & Play** (Two Players) option in settings to play against a friend locally on the same computer.

### How are game sessions saved?
All games and match logs are saved automatically to your Mac's local sandbox storage. When you quit and reopen the app, your progress is automatically restored.
