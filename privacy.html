Privacy Policy

Long Backgammon for macOS

Privacy Overview

Effective date: October 2026

We respect your privacy. Long Backgammon does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information or usage data.

Data Usage & Collection

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal details such as your name, email address, physical location, or device identifiers.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The application contains zero third-party tracking libraries, analytics tools, or advertising SDKs.
  • Local Storage: Your game scores, settings, and current board state are stored solely on your Mac using standard Apple Sandboxed storage (UserDefaults and local JSON files). This data never leaves your computer.
  • No Network Transmission: The application operates fully offline. It does not send any telemetry, analytics, or background requests over the internet.

Children's Privacy

Because Long Backgammon does not collect any personal data, it complies with privacy laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us at longbackgammon@icloud.com.
