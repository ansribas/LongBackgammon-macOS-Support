❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are the rules of Long Backgammon?
Long Backgammon (Nardi) is played with 15 checkers on a 24-point board. Unlike short backgammon, there is no hitting (checkers cannot be knocked off). Only one checker can be taken from the "head" (starting point) per turn, except for certain initial doubles (3:3, 4:4, 6:6). Checkers move counter-clockwise around the board toward your home quarter to be borne off.
How can I switch themes or adjust game speed?
Click the Settings button (or press Command+,) to open the preferences window. You can switch between Classic Wood and Elegant Marble boards, select checker movement trajectories (straight or arc), and choose between fast and relaxed animation speeds.
Can I play with another player on the same Mac?
Yes! Select the Pass & Play (Two Players) mode in the game settings or when starting a new game to take turns on the same Mac.
Does the game save my progress automatically?
Yes, your current game state and moves are automatically saved to your Mac's secure sandbox. You can quit the app at any time and resume right where you left off.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

Your privacy is paramount. Long Backgammon is built strictly adhering to Apple's App Sandbox and privacy standards:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, request, transmit, or store any personal information, identifiers, or contacts.
  • No Tracking & No Ads: The app does not track you across other apps or websites, does not use ad identifiers (IDFA), and contains no third-party tracking frameworks.
  • Local Storage Only: Game settings, logs, and statistics are stored exclusively locally on your device within the sandboxed Application Support directory.
  • No External Servers: The game functions 100% offline and makes no external network calls during gameplay.
